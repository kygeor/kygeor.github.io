I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the [Division of Applied Mathematics](https://appliedmath.brown.edu/) at Brown University, as a member of the [CRUNCH Group](https://sites.brown.edu/crunch-group/) led by Prof. George Em. Karniadakis.

From 2024 to 2026 I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) in the [laboratory of Prof. Constantinos Siettos](https://www.siettos.net/research-group) at the University of Naples Federico II, under the Future of Artificial Intelligence Research ([FAIR](https://fondazione-fair.it/en/)) fellowship. My research there focused on machine learning and numerical analysis for PDEs and dynamical systems.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Statistics at the Athens University of Economics and Business, under the supervision of Prof. Athanasios N. Giannakopoulos, with the thesis _"Discrete, continuous, and machine learning models and applications in credit risk"_ ([full text](https://www.dept.aueb.gr/sites/default/files/stat/phd/Georgiou%20PhD.pdf)).

I hold a BSc in Pure Mathematics from Imperial College London with First Class Honours and an MSc in Stochastic/Financial Mathematics from the LSE.

I am also a co-founder and currently Scientific Advisor of the startup [finloup](https://finloup.com/), where I led the development of proprietary statistical and machine learning models for credit risk modelling using banking transactional data. For this work, I was included in the Forbes 30 Under 30 list for Greece ([article](https://www.forbesgreece.gr/forbes-30-under-30-2023/3678020/to-mathimatiko-mualo-tis-finloup)).
