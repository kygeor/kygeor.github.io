## The Erevna Podcast

Along with two collaborators, I founded and host the [Erevna podcast](https://www.youtube.com/@erevna_science), with discussions with academics, students and professionals about scientific knowledge, education and real-world applications. It is a small, fun project that we keep up as much as we can, so if you have interesting topics or guests to propose, please feel free to reach out.
