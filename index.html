Kyriakos C. Georgiou

Postdoctoral Research Associate, Division of Applied Mathematics, Brown University

Division of Applied Mathematics

Brown University

Providence, RI, USA

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Division of Applied Mathematics at Brown University, as a member of the CRUNCH Group led by Prof. George Em. Karniadakis.

From 2024 to 2026 I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) in the laboratory of Prof. Constantinos Siettos at the University of Naples Federico II, under the Future of Artificial Intelligence Research (FAIR) fellowship. My research there focused on machine learning and numerical analysis for PDEs and dynamical systems.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Statistics at the Athens University of Economics and Business, under the supervision of Prof. Athanasios N. Giannakopoulos, with the thesis “Discrete, continuous, and machine learning models and applications in credit risk” (full text).

I hold a BSc in Pure Mathematics from Imperial College London with First Class Honours and an MSc in Stochastic/Financial Mathematics from the LSE.

I am also a co-founder and currently Chief AI Officer of the startup finloup, where I lead the AI infrastructure of the company for the assessment and monitoring of credit risk and the development of proprietary statistical and machine learning models for credit risk modelling using banking transactional data. For this work, I was included in the Forbes 30 Under 30 list for Greece (article).
